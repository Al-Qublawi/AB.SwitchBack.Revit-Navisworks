AB SwitchBack - Privacy Policy

Publisher: Abdullah Lotfy · Version 1.0 · Effective 24 November 2026

AB SwitchBack is a Revit add-in and a Navisworks plugin that work as a pair: a click in Navisworks selects the same element in Revit. This policy explains what data they handle and where it goes. It applies to the version distributed through the Autodesk Design and Make Marketplace.

In short: AB SwitchBack sends nothing over the internet. The publisher operates no servers and receives no data about you, your computer or your projects. Revit and Navisworks talk to each other only on your own computer.

1. What the add-in handles, and where it stays

DataWhat it is used forWhere it is kept
The Revit element id of the item you click in Navisworks, and the reply from Revit To select that element in Revit Passed between Revit and Navisworks through a Windows named pipe that only processes on your computer can open. Not stored.
One small file per running Revit or Navisworks session: process id, application and version, the title of the open document, and the pipe name So Navisworks can find the running Revit sessions and offer them as targets %LOCALAPPDATA%\ABSwitchBack\instances. Deleted when the session closes.
Your settings The trigger gesture and what Revit does with an element %LOCALAPPDATA%\ABSwitchBack\config.txt
Log files: element ids and names, document titles, timings and any error messages To diagnose a problem, if you choose to send them %LOCALAPPDATA%\ABSwitchBack\logs and %LOCALAPPDATA%\AB Adv Tools\Logs

2. What is not collected

  • No internet connection is made. The Marketplace version has no update check, no telemetry, no analytics and no licence check.
  • No personal information: not your name, e-mail address, Autodesk account or computer name.
  • Nothing from your models is copied anywhere.

3. Sharing

None. Nothing is sent to the publisher or to anyone else. If you e-mail log files for support, they are used only to answer your question and are deleted when it is resolved.

4. Keeping and removing your data

Everything listed above is on your own computer, and you may delete it at any time: remove the %LOCALAPPDATA%\ABSwitchBack and %LOCALAPPDATA%\AB Adv Tools folders. Uninstalling AB SwitchBack removes the program, not these folders.

5. Changes and contact

A change to this policy is published with a new version of the add-in, with a new version number and date above. Questions: Abdullah Lotfy, abdullah.mech93@gmail.com.
